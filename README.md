## 1. BrewMetrics Analytics: Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales data—focusing on revenue trends, order volume, product category performance, time-based sales patterns, and store-level comparisons.

---

## 2. Purpose

The BrewMetrics Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI report built to monitor and evaluate coffee shop sales performance across multiple dimensions. It provides actionable insights into customer purchasing behavior, peak sales periods, product demand, and store performance—enabling business owners and stakeholders to make data-driven operational and strategic decisions.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – For building interactive dashboards and visual analytics
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for cleaning, transforming, and preparing transactional sales data
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Created KPIs, month-over-month growth metrics, averages, and dynamic measures
* 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Relationships established between sales, products, dates, and store locations
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for portfolio presentation

---

## 4. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Coffee shops generate large volumes of sales data daily, but without effective visualization, it becomes difficult to answer key business questions such as:

* Which products and categories drive the most revenue?
* What are the peak sales hours and days?
* How do weekday and weekend sales compare?
* Which store locations perform best over time?

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To provide a unified analytics solution that:

* Delivers a high-level overview of sales performance
* Reveals trends by time, product category, and store location
* Supports staffing, inventory, and promotional planning
* Helps identify growth opportunities and operational inefficiencies

---

### • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

#### **Key KPIs (Top Summary Cards)**

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold
* Month-over-Month Sales Growth

---

#### **Sales Overview Analysis**

**Daily Sales Trend (Column Chart)**
Displays sales performance over time with an average sales reference line to identify high- and low-performing days.

**Weekday vs Weekend Sales (Donut Chart)**
Compares revenue distribution between weekdays and weekends to highlight customer purchasing behavior.

**Sales by Product Category (Bar Charts)**
Analyzes revenue contribution from categories such as Coffee, Tea, Bakery, and Chocolate, including sub-category breakdowns.

**Store Location Performance (Bar Chart)**
Compares sales across store locations to identify top-performing and underperforming branches.

---

#### **Time-Based Analysis**

**Sales by Day & Hour (Heatmap)**
Visualizes sales intensity across days of the week and hours of the day, helping identify:

* Peak sales hours
* Weekend demand surges
* Low-traffic periods for optimization

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

* **Operational Efficiency:** Identifies peak sales periods to optimize staffing levels
* **Product Optimization:** Highlights top-selling products and categories for better inventory planning
* **Location Intelligence:** Enables performance comparison across store locations
* **Revenue Growth:** Supports targeted promotions and pricing strategies
* **Strategic Decision-Making:** Converts raw sales data into clear, actionable insights
